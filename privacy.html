Privacy niyam

KirayaSetu sirf kirayedar aur makaan malik ko jodta hai. Hum aapka data bechte nahi.

Hum kya rakhte hain

Google login se aapka naam, email aur profile photo. Aapka mobile number aur shehar jo aap khud daalte ho. Makaan malik ke kamre ki photo, video, kiraya aur kamre ki GPS location.

Kaun kya dekh sakta hai

Kamre ki photo, kiraya aur lagbhag 500 meter ka area sabko dikhta hai. Makaan malik ka mobile number aur ghar ka exact pata sirf us kirayedar ko dikhta hai jiski request makaan malik ne khud approve ki ho. Kirayedar ka number bhi sirf approve ke baad makaan malik ko dikhta hai.

Internet call

App ke andar ki call seedhe dono phones ke beech hoti hai. Koi number share nahi hota aur call record nahi hoti.

Awaaz se form

Jab makaan malik bol ke kamra daalta hai, woh text form bharne ke liye Google Gemini AI ko bheja jata hai. Awaaz khud save nahi hoti.

Data hatana

Aap app mein apna kamra kabhi bhi hata sakte ho. Poora account aur saara data hatane ke liye KirayaSetu team se sampark karo — hum 7 din mein hata denge.
